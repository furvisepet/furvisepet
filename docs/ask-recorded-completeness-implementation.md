## Supported meaning and positive proof

The exact unit is a distinct, persisted episode ID in the retained **care-entry and semantic-claim episode register**, for one current owned pet, one canonical symptom concept and one half-open episode-start period (or all retained dates). It is not literal lifetime coverage, a count of notes, maximum sequence number, or a claim about unrecorded life. Conversation-only reports and memory-only facts are outside this narrow register. Their imported semantic claims, when present, cannot be silently ignored.

This deliberately strict first supported scope requires every retained source in the requested period, plus every member of each scoped group, to be accounted for. All must have readable, current owned membership and supported classification. Onsets must be whole notes of the form `Milo had a new vomiting episode.` (also started/began, separate/recurrent and optional a/an/terminal period). A continuation must be exactly `Milo continued the same vomiting episode.` with continuation membership. Names and supported topics are substituted literally. Every group needs exactly one deduplicated onset, and its time must equal the episode start. Free-form general notes, extra qualifications, other lifecycle roles, generic onsets without an explicit separate/new/recurrent boundary, mixed-concept sources in the census, unknown subject claims, unresolved imports and ambiguous grouping return unknown. They are not classified by a model or simply dropped to obtain a total.

A recorded count can be zero only with an authoritative empty retained census. Deleted sources, forgotten claims and correction effects cannot establish zero by disappearing. Hard removals after draft installation leave conservative owner-wide removal debt. There is deliberately no automatic debt reset, merge/split alias, reassignment recovery or re-import repair path; such inventories remain unknown. Retained historical deletion/tombstone/missing-source indicators are also revalidated. This is a bounded supported scope, not a general-notes completeness solver.

Before production edits, `scripts/audits/ask-recorded-completeness.cases.mjs` executed the unchanged harness through `generateAskHistoryAnswer`, provider-input construction, and the final validator. Its positive native-care inventory failed **1/1**, with `null !== 1` (`docs/ask-recorded-before.log`). After implementation it returns **Exactly 1 recorded vomiting episode**, preserving stored sequence 7 and displayed ordinal 1 and rejecting the provider's invented seven lifetime episodes. A separate case returns **12 exact recorded episodes**, renders eight, validates the undisplayed groups, and successfully resolves a saved reference from the eight-item page. Native claim-only, imported-care/claim deduplication and PostgreSQL offset timestamps exactly at the inclusive period start also pass. These are real callback executions with mocked database/provider dependencies; they are not SQL or live-provider proof.

## Implementation

- `app/lib/intelligence/recorded-inventory.ts`: validates the versioned census, current owner/pet/concept/period, safe bounds, unique IDs, a nonzero decimal revision, and a statement timestamp within 30 seconds of application time. Matches every raw member to the census before exact admission; applies the narrow semantic grammar.
- `app/lib/intelligence/episode-history.ts`: validates the entire inventory before taking the first eight display items. The exact aggregate is computed from the full, validated set of stable episode IDs. Requires complete whole-group validation, imported-lineage deduplication, correction closure and exact census agreement. An absent, failed or incompatible inventory leaves totals null. Captures the initial payload digest before subsequent reads and brackets **both** correction passes with a final inventory read. Any changed revision or payload rejects exact authority.
- `app/lib/intelligence/history-retrieval.ts`: retains the full correction-graph digest for read revalidation, and adds source-specific connected-component hashes for saved references. Each component includes claims, relations, lineage, source versions and removal targets. Unrelated undisplayed groups no longer invalidate an unchanged displayed reference. Pre-change saved hashes may fail closed once and require a fresh list; IDs are never substituted.
- `app/lib/intelligence/episode-contract.ts`: types exact recorded totals and emits explicit register/period/as-of wording in the existing production final validator. Safety precedence, read-only evidence contracts, rejected care actions/learnings and final-render reference attachment remain intact. Saved references retain the existing bounded `partial`/`exactTotal:null` contract: a follow-up revalidates the selected source, never reuses a stored exact count as fresh inventory authority.
- `supabase/drafts/ask_recorded_completeness.sql`: self-contained unapplied draft using the actual membership reader body under a private batch function, plus the public reader wrapper, transactional revision, removal debt, RLS and restricted grants. This is not a fabricated migration filename or a claim of CLI success.
- `supabase/drafts/ask_recorded_completeness.rollback.sql`: restores the exact separately validated membership reader and removes the new functions/tables/triggers.
- `supabase/tests/ask_recorded_completeness.sql`: rollback-only assertions for real source writers/importer, full census beyond the display page, period/owner/pet/concept scope, native positive inventory, partial/full imports and source hashes, new/unclassified sources, correction, soft/hard removal, forgetting, overflow, snapshot consistency, revision rollback, private grants and trigger coverage.
- `supabase/tests/ask_recorded_completeness_reversal.sql`: actual reversal body within an outer rollback-only transaction, with function/table/permission assertions.
- `scripts/audits/ask-recorded-completeness.cases.mjs` and `tests/ask-recorded-completeness.test.mjs`: 34 callback cases and a default-suite wrapper. Original audits, fixtures, harnesses and tests were not edited.

## Snapshot and write authority

Every new reader function is STABLE with fixed `search_path=pg_catalog` and schema-qualified relations/functions. All internal membership batches, census queries and revision reads use the calling PostgreSQL statement's MVCC snapshot. The aggregate input and display source material are therefore from one statement snapshot. Application-side correction reads are separate statements; equality of the **transactionally updated** revision before and after all those reads ensures no contributing committed mutation was admitted between them. A change committed after the final read does not retroactively change the answer's stated as-of inventory. A sequence was not used: sequence advancement could become visible before the source transaction committed and would not roll back with it.

The draft adds row triggers to these actual contributing tables: `dog_profiles`, `pet_care_entries`, `pet_care_episodes`, `pet_care_episode_events`, `semantic_claims`, `semantic_claim_relations`, `semantic_claim_legacy_lineage`, `ask_history_removed_relation_targets`, `semantic_concepts`, `semantic_concept_aliases`, `ask_conversation_messages`, `ask_conversations`, `furvise_memories`, and `dog_memories`. Insert/update/delete, source corrections, ownership changes, import batches, projection rebuilds, edge removals, tombstones, forgetting, and deletion cascades all advance the same transactional revision. TRUNCATE is explicitly rejected while this draft is installed so bulk removal cannot bypass row-level invalidation/debt. Account deletion does not create a new debt FK to an already deleted auth user.

Writer/schema inspection covered the generic semantic care writer (`20260807010000`), reconciliation (`20260808020000`, `20260808030000`), lifecycle membership/rebuild/removal (`20260810230000`, `20260811120000`), governed claims/membership (`20260811150000`), import/registry/lineage (`20260812044302`), scoped correction reader and removed-edge/lineage tombstones (`20260905045120`), care-entry routes/update/remove RPC callers, conversation removal, canonical memory saves, semantic persistence, and v2 lifecycle compatibility/projection rebuilding. Their underlying writes are intercepted at the actual tables, including indirect trigger writes; application callers are not relied on to remember to invalidate. Existing import source hashes include the care row, pet species, and care membership role, exactly as in the importer and validated membership reader.

Native unimported care is supported. Once any care import lineage exists for the pet, every scoped care source needs its primary imported claim represented in the census; partial frontiers fail closed. Claim-only imports still need their authoritative source and matching hash/lineage. Ask-message claims require a retained user message and conversation owned by the authenticated owner. Other pets/owners never enter the payload.

The global revision serializes contributing writers, even across owners. This is a deliberately conservative correctness tradeoff with potential contention, unnecessary read invalidation and deadlock retries; it is **not scale-validated**. Removal debt is also conservative across the owner's pets and periods. A later optimization must preserve transactional invalidation and cannot silently clear debt. Administrative trigger disabling or superuser data rewriting is outside the application authority contract.

The revision/debt tables have RLS enabled and no application table grants. The private membership batch and trigger functions are not executable by PUBLIC, anon, authenticated or service_role. Only authenticated may execute the public owner-scoped reader. No existing RLS is weakened; there is no application service-role fallback.

## Bounds

- One shared five-second episode-phase deadline. Complete inventory: three public source RPCs and at most six correction-page calls per pass, two passes: **15 RPCs maximum**. Pinned follow-up: existing saved-reference RPC, two source RPCs, and up to twelve graph calls. The preceding historical phase retains its separate five-second budget, candidate-page and graph limits.
- SQL enforces an existing nonzero statement timeout of at most eight seconds; the client deadline can abort earlier. Each public list call makes the existing initial bounded membership probe and at most four private eight-episode batches.
- At most 32 episodes are admitted; a 33rd is an overflow sentinel. Census probes take at most 65 care IDs and 65 claim IDs; a combined total over 64 rejects completeness. Complete client payloads contain at most 64 raw members/sources across the register, at most eight members per group. SQL batches can temporarily inspect up to 288 raw edges (four batches of 8 x 9) before overflow rejects them and returns the legacy bounded subset. No unbounded payload is returned.
- The exact count is computed before the eight-item display slice. Raw sequence is preserved separately from display ordinal. Membership, sources and claims must match the same census; an invalid ninth group cannot hide behind paging.
- Existing payload caps remain 2,000 note characters, 200 title characters and 16,000 bytes per claim; correction closure retains its 64-root, 128-claim/relation and six-call bounds per pass. Component hashing is bounded by these graph/source limits.
- The SQL census is index/timeout bounded, not proven constant-time. Query plans, broad owner histories, concurrent writer contention and timeout behavior need supervisor measurement. Clock skew over 30 seconds fails closed. No database performance claims are inferred from JS fixtures.
