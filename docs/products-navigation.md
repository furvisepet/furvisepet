# Products navigation readiness

Products remains available at `/shop` and through relevant care flows, but it is intentionally secondary while catalog access is limited.

Restore Products to primary desktop and mobile navigation only after all of these conditions are met:

- an authorized provider is connected;
- Canada coverage is meaningful;
- category coverage is sufficient for everyday browsing;
- live retailer destinations have been verified.

Until then, keep the route, search, catalog ingestion, and contextual links active. The account menu's **Browse products** link is the general secondary entry point.
