# Furvise SEO content strategy V1

This is a planning document for after beta data demonstrates that Furvise creates durable value and retention. It does not authorize new public routes in V1.

## Editorial principle

Furvise should earn search visibility by publishing genuinely useful resources with a clear human purpose and a Furvise-specific perspective. The strategy is not a scaled keyword factory. Publication volume is less important than original usefulness, accurate claims, and a clear connection to Furvise's category: understanding a pet over time.

Every future resource must:

- answer a real pet-owner question with original value;
- explain why continuity, change over time, and connected history matter;
- be factually reviewed when veterinary topics are involved;
- avoid diagnosis, treatment claims, and AI-vet positioning;
- avoid synthetic testimonials, fake citations, or inflated product claims;
- have a distinct purpose instead of repeating another page for a keyword variation.

## Recommended future resources

### 1. Why your pet's history matters before a vet visit

Help owners understand which changes, dates, routines, and recurrences can make a veterinary conversation clearer. Show the practical value of arriving with a connected story without implying diagnosis.

### 2. What to remember when something changes with your pet

Offer a calm framework for noting what changed, when it started, what improved or worsened, and what is worth bringing to a veterinarian. Keep emergency escalation guidance prominent where relevant.

### 3. How to prepare your pet's story for a veterinarian

Explain how to turn scattered observations into a concise timeline. Connect the resource to Vet Brief only when the shipped feature and entitlement remain accurate.

### 4. Why repeated pet symptoms are easier to understand with context

Explain recurrence and change over time in plain language. This resource would require veterinary factual review and careful wording that does not turn Furvise into a symptom checker.

### 5. How Furvise differs from keeping pet notes in your phone

Describe the difference between disconnected notes and an evolving, pet-specific history that can inform future questions. Focus on continuity and correct-pet context rather than claiming that memory alone is the product.

## Publication gate

Do not build these pages until beta evidence supports the underlying user need and the team can maintain editorial accuracy. Before publication, each resource needs a named human owner, factual review where applicable, a unique search intent, canonical metadata, inclusion in navigation where useful, and a meaningful sitemap date.
